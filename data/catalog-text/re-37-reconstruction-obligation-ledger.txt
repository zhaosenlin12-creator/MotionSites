Keep every assumption a rebuild rests on in one auditable table. Create a ledger with columns: claim id, claim text, category (structure, token, motion, media, network, behaviour), status of observed, inferred or unknown, evidence pointer (capture id, step id, selector, byte range), a confidence note, and the single capture that would upgrade an unknown to observed. Fill it from existing evidence before writing code: a computed style read on the live page is observed, a duration guessed from a minified variable name is inferred, an interaction nobody triggered is unknown. For each unknown state the cheapest settling probe, such as one more scenario step, a longer observation window, a second viewport, or a request body. Let no claim in status unknown be consumed by the build without a visible fallback, and record the number you chose when you hardcode one. Re-run the capture after each probe so rows change state through a diff rather than from memory. Do not close a gap by asserting access you do not have: a login wall, a paywall or a protected stream stays unknown and its row says why. Rebuild stack: one JSON ledger committed beside the code, a rule that every token and timeline value cites a claim id, and a build check that fails when an unknown claim is used without a fallback. Acceptance: every style token and timeline number maps to a claim id, no unknown appears as fact, observed, inferred and unknown counts print on every build, and each unknown names one concrete capture to upgrade it.
