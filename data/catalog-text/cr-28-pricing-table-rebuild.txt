Rebuild a pricing section as three measured parts: plan cards, a comparison grid, and a billing toggle. Start with the cards. Record each plan's slots (name, price, unit, one-line claim, four to six feature rows, action label), the inner padding, the gap between cards, and how the recommended plan differs: an accent border at 1 px, a label at 12 px above the name, an 8 px lift that changes no column width. Use one radius family, 8 px on cards, 6 px on the toggle and buttons, 4 px on feature rows. Then the toggle: two labels, a slider that moves in 180 ms on the standard ease, and a price swap that changes digits only, so nothing above or below the row moves; reserve the widest value with tabular figures and a min-width on the price element. Then the comparison grid: eight rows maximum, feature column at 40 percent, one column per plan, row separators at 12 percent alpha, sticky row labels, and a collapse rule at 640 px that keeps the feature name, the recommended plan, and a disclosure per row. Acceptance: toggling at 1440, 1024 and 390 px moves no element by more than 1 px; the recommended card is unique and labelled in markup, not by colour alone; the grid never scrolls sideways at 390 px; every price cell uses tabular figures; keyboard focus reaches the toggle and every action with a 3 px halo.
