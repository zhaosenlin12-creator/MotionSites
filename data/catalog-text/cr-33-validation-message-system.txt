Write the validation system before writing the form, because the timing decides whether the form feels calm or abusive. Rules first: validate on blur and on submit, never on keystroke, except a confirmation field, which may check per keystroke after its first submit failure. Clear an error the moment the field becomes valid on blur, not while typing. Give each message a rule id, a field, a trigger, the condition, the text and a severity. Then placement: reserve a 20 px message row under every field at all times so showing an error moves nothing; the message sits 6 px below the field, renders at 12 px, and carries a non-colour cue, an icon plus the word that failed, because colour alone is invisible to part of the audience. Then announcement: mark the field invalid, point it at its message with a described-by relation, move focus to the first invalid field on submit, and send one summary through a polite live region, once per submit rather than once per field. Error text says what to do in under 60 characters, in sentence case, without blame. Acceptance: submitting an empty form moves no element and announces a single summary; each message clears on its stated trigger; the reserved row keeps the submit button at the same y position with zero and with five errors; the announced output names field and message; error text reaches 4.5 to 1 and the invalid border 3 to 1.
