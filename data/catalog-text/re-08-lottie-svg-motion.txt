Goal: recover vector motion exactly instead of eyeballing it. First check the network log for animation payloads: filter responses by .json, .lottie and .js with bodymovin markers, then inspect a body for the Lottie schema keys v, fr, ip, op, w, h, layers, assets and shapes. Save the raw JSON and record fr (frame rate), op minus ip (duration in frames) and the player version exposed by window.lottie or the bundle. Second, for hand-authored SVG motion inspect the DOM: read each path's d, viewBox, stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset, and look for SMIL animate and animateTransform elements, CSS keyframes on path elements, or GSAP DrawSVG with a stroke-dasharray equal to getTotalLength(). Third, measure the rhythm: call getAnimations() on the SVG nodes for delays and easings, and capture a ten-frame filmstrip to confirm the visual order of the reveal. Rebuild stack: lottie-web with the SVG renderer and the original fr preserved, or a hand-authored SVG whose path length and stroke-dashoffset keyframes are generated from getTotalLength(), triggered by an IntersectionObserver. Acceptance: total duration matches within one frame, seeking to frame 30 and 60 matches the capture, there is no layout shift when the animation resolves, and the Lottie file is stored locally with its licence and source URL noted.
