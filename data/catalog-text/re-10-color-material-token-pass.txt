Goal: derive a page's real visual system instead of sampling a few hex codes. Step 1 — walk every rendered element, roughly two thousand on a typical landing page, and collect from getComputedStyle: color, backgroundColor, every border colour, backgroundImage, boxShadow, filter, backdropFilter, borderRadius and opacity. Skip display: none subtrees. Step 2 — weight colours by painted area rather than count: approximate each element's area as clientWidth times clientHeight, then cluster the resulting colour-and-area list into eight to fourteen swatches, separating text, surface, accent and border roles and keeping the top five per role. Step 3 — parse backgroundImage strings for gradients and record direction, stop positions and colour stops per gradient; group repeated shadow strings into an elevation ramp by blur and spread; identify glass surfaces as backdrop-filter blur plus a low-alpha white or black fill plus a one-pixel light border. Step 4 — verify against pixels: take a viewport screenshot, sample it with a canvas getImageData over each swatch, and discard any swatch that differs beyond a just-noticeable delta. Rebuild stack: a tokens.css theme of semantic custom properties (--surface-1, --text-muted, --accent, --shadow-2, --glass-blur) mapped into the project's variables or Tailwind theme, with a light-mode counterpart derived or explicitly marked absent. Acceptance: every swatch matches its screenshot sample within delta-E 3, gradients keep their original angle and stop offsets, and no unnamed raw hex appears outside the palette block.
