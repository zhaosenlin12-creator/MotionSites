Fix card nesting with three rules a reviewer can verify in one glance. Rule one, one radius family: outer containers 8 px, cards inside them 6 px, controls inside those 4 px, pills fully round, and no two radii at the same depth. Rule two, inset by exactly one spacing step, 8 or 12 px, and shift the inner surface one step in luminance rather than using both a lighter fill and a border. Rule three, at most two visible borders along any line from page edge to text: a bordered card inside a bordered card is exactly the failure this prevents. Depth comes from one light model: at rest a shadow of y 1 px, blur 2 px, alpha 12 percent; for the single floating element per view, y 8 px, blur 24 px, alpha 18 percent. Remove shadows from anything that already has a border, and never fake elevation with a border alone. Then audit by counting: for each card record depth level, radius, fill, border, shadow and inner padding, and read the table top to bottom. Acceptance: nothing sits at depth three or deeper without a written reason; a 4x corner crop shows no double outline; every nested padding value comes from the 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 ladder; text contrast holds at 4.5 to 1 at every nesting level; shadow offsets are identical on every surface.
