Turn a screenshot set into the design tokens the target uses, before styling a single component. Work in this order. Evidence: for each of the three widths, crop the page into regions (header, hero, cards, footer) and sample the dominant fill, text colour and border colour per region; record hex values, never names. Type: measure pixel height of the top heading, subheads, body and caption, plus line-height from baseline to baseline, weight and letter-spacing; note the font family and its fallback stack. Space: measure gaps between blocks, card padding, grid gutters and the content column max-width; snap to the nearest 4 px step while keeping the measured value in a comment. Shape: radii for cards, buttons, inputs and pills; border widths; shadow offset, blur, spread and alpha. Emit tokens.css with custom properties for colour, type scale, space, radius, shadow and container width, plus tokens.json holding value, role and the crop each came from. Compute contrast for every text-on-surface pair and fail anything below 4.5 to 1 for body text and 3 to 1 for large text or controls; where the original itself fails, keep the measured token but add a compliant alternative. Acceptance self-tests: grepping the build for hex colours or raw px outside tokens.css returns nothing; every token in tokens.json is used at least once; rendered heading sizes and section padding match the measurements within 2 px at each width.
