Fix a seven-step type ramp and one leading ratio, then reject any text that is not on it. Choose the steps 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 20 and 26 px and give each a job in the token file: 11 and 12 for dense metadata, timestamps and captions, 13 for labels and list rows, 15 for body copy, 17 for a lead paragraph, 20 for card and section headings, 26 for the page title. State that a single screen may use at most four steps. Derive line height as 1.45 times the size for anything that wraps, and a fixed 1.2 for single-line labels, button text and headings; round both to whole pixels so glyphs keep sharp edges, and expose --text-n-size and --text-n-leading together. Set a measure of 62 to 70 characters by capping the lead at 34em and body at 38em, and write the rule that a line-height literal never appears outside the token file. Build a specimen page showing one paragraph at every step with its wrapped line count printed beside it, plus a long unbroken token, a 14-character compound and a string of digits to prove the wrap and the number spacing hold. Then set the same copy at 1440 and at 390 px. Acceptance: exactly seven sizes exist; no rendered text sits outside the ramp; each wrapped block measures within 1 px of 1.45 times its size; no line exceeds 76 characters; the specimen shows no clipped descender at any step.
