Build a deck of five cards that each flip between a front and a back face when activated. Card 320x420px, 18px radius, 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.1) border, #1c1f28 fill, stacked with an 18px vertical offset and a 2deg rotation per depth level so the deck's edge is visible. Inside each card place an .inner with transform-style: preserve-3d and a 700ms transform transition on cubic-bezier(0.5,0.05,0.2,1.02); flipping to rotateY(180deg) reveals the back face, which carries backface-visibility: hidden and is pre-rotated 180deg so its text is never seen mirrored. Set perspective 1200px on the card rather than the deck, and give .inner translateZ(0) to force a composited layer. After the transition lands, run a 90ms spring settle from 176deg to 180deg so the flip reads as physical; drive it from the transitionend event rather than a hardcoded timeout. Each card is a real button with aria-pressed, a 2px visible focus ring and keyboard activation on Enter and Space; on activation move focus to the revealed heading and let Escape flip back and return focus to the button. Tap flips on coarse pointers, but a drag over 12px cancels the flip so vertical swipes still scroll the page. Fallback when 3D transforms are unsupported or prefers-reduced-motion is set: stack both faces in one grid cell, crossfade content over 200ms and rotate nothing. Acceptance: no mirrored text at any angle, flip twice returns to the exact front state, the settled transform is an exact matrix not a drifting angle, and hidden faces are never focusable or announced.
