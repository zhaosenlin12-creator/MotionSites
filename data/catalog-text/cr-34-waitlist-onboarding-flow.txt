Lay out the waitlist or onboarding flow as numbered steps with exactly one action each. Define the steps first: entry with an email field, confirmation that the address was accepted, a profile step with two fields at most, and a done state that says what happens next and when. Write recovery for every failure before drawing the success path: invalid address, already subscribed, rate limited, network failure, expired token. Each gets its own message, one retry path and a support link, and no failure may leave the person on an unchanged screen. The end state is explicit: a dashboard link or a dated promise, never an empty success page with a checkmark. Cap the flow at four steps and two fields per step, and make each step fit one viewport at 390 px with its action visible without scrolling. Keep the whole flow on one route so a reload resumes at the current step rather than resetting.
