Audit contrast as three ladders and report replacements rather than warnings. Ladder one, non-text at 3 to 1: field borders under the pointer, focus halos, icons that carry meaning, chart strokes, toggle tracks, scrollbar thumbs. Ladder two, body text at 4.5 to 1: paragraph copy, labels, table cells, and button text in every state including hover and disabled. Ladder three, small print at 7 to 1: anything at 12 px or below, captions, legal lines, timestamps, and text over imagery. Collect the pairs the page actually renders from computed styles, not from a palette file, and include pairs that only exist in hover, focus, disabled, error and glass-over-media states, since those are what an audit normally misses. Compute each ratio, then for every failure write one line: element, foreground, background, measured ratio, required ratio, and the nearest passing token already on the scale with its ratio. Change tokens rather than components, so one token fix closes several pairs. Run the whole audit in both themes and report them side by side. Acceptance: every failing pair names a replacement token and its ratio; no replacement creates a pair below its own threshold; a rerun after the fixes reports zero failures in both themes; text over media is measured against its worst-case scrim; disabled controls are listed separately instead of being silently exempted.
