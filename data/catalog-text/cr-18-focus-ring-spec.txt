Specify a single focus treatment and hold every control to it. Draw it in two parts: a 2 px hairline in the accent ink following the control's own radius, plus a 3 px halo outside it at about 35 percent alpha, so the ring reads at 1440 px and is never clipped by an overflow container. Fix the geometry: hairline inset 0, halo outside by 3 px, no layout growth, no offset drift on rounded corners, and a ring that stays whole when two controls sit 4 px apart. Set the target at 3 to 1 for the hairline against the surface directly outside the control, and list the surface pairs that fail, replacing the accent per surface rather than shipping a ring nobody can see. Differentiate the two families: buttons, toggles and links take the full ring on :focus-visible only, while text fields and selects show it on :focus and simultaneously switch their edge to the interactive colour, because a field is a place to type rather than something to press. Keep the ring visible through hover and active, and never ease it longer than 120 ms. Then audit by keyboard at both themes and at 200 percent zoom. Acceptance: Tab reaches every interactive element in visual order with a visible ring; no ring is clipped or shifts layout; measured contrast is at least 3 to 1 on all five surfaces in both themes; Enter and Space still activate while the ring shows; a pointer-only run leaves no ring behind.
