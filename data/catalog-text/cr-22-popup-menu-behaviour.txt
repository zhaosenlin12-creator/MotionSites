Pin down how a dropdown opens, closes and behaves when the viewport is short. Open it with a 120 ms fade and no scale, rows already in place so the list never appears to grow; close in 90 ms and unmount only after the fade ends. Size the panel to its widest row plus 16 px of padding and never to full width, cap the height at nine 32 px rows, 288 px plus 8 px of padding, and scroll past that with a slim thumb. Place it 4 px below the trigger; when less than the panel height plus 12 px remains below, flip above and keep the same gap; when neither side fits, open at the larger side, scroll from the top and keep the trigger in view. On open, move focus to the selected row without scrolling it into the middle, let arrows traverse rows and wrap at the ends, send Home and End to the extremes, close on Escape and return focus to the trigger, and close on an outside pointer-down without swallowing that click. Mark the trigger with aria-expanded and aria-haspopup, give the active row aria-selected, and use real menu rows rather than restyled buttons. Then drive it with the keyboard in a 320 px tall window. Acceptance: measured fade times land within 10 ms of spec; exactly nine rows show before scrolling; panel width equals widest row plus 16 px; focus returns to the trigger after Escape and after an outside click; the panel opens fully inside the viewport at every tested height.
