Derive a motion system from evidence instead of guessing durations. Screen-record the target, step frames, and measure the enter duration, exit duration, delay between siblings, distance travelled, and whether opacity, transform or layout animates. Sort the measurements into token tiers: instant 80 to 120 ms for press feedback, control 150 to 200 ms for hover and focus, enter 240 to 320 ms for panels and modals, exit about 65 percent of enter, and ambient loops 6 to 12 s. Define easing tokens: standard for in-place change, decelerate for entrances, accelerate for exits, and a spring for draggable elements with explicit stiffness and damping. Express stagger as a per-item delay with a total cap. Implement with CSS transitions and keyframes, animating transform, opacity and colour only, and add a reduced-motion branch that keeps state changes while removing travel and loops. Acceptance self-tests: every token is exercised by a named element in a motion manifest; a timeline dump of the rebuilt interaction matches the measured durations within 20 percent; nothing animates width, height, top or left; there is no layout shift after load; with prefers-reduced-motion enabled all content stays reachable and no loop runs; a frame check shows no long task during the heaviest entrance.
