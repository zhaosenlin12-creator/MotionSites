Assemble a field from named parts and fix where each one sits. The parts are label at 13 px, 4 px above the control, always visible and never replaced by a placeholder; control at 40 px with 12 px side padding, a 6 px radius and 15 px text; hint at 12 px muted, 4 px below the control; error at 12 px in the danger ink with a 14 px icon, taking the hint's slot; and success in the same slot, accent ink, one word. Reserve 18 px under every field for whichever of hint, error or success is showing, so validation never moves the form. Combine states on purpose: focus draws the accent edge plus the halo and keeps the error text visible when the value is still invalid, while error without focus holds a 2 px danger edge. Validate on blur for the first attempt and on input only after the first failure, and never disable the submit button as a substitute for explaining what is wrong. Handle autofill by repainting the browser's autofill fill to the field colour so contrast survives, and long values by ellipsis in read mode with the full value in edit mode. Then test an 80-character value, a paste, an autofilled email and a screen reader pass. Acceptance: all five parts exist for every field; error and success swap with zero layout shift; no invalid state relies on colour alone; autofill keeps field contrast above 4.5 to 1; a keyboard-only submit reaches every hint and error through aria-describedby.
