Goal: reproduce a site's pointer feel, not just its look. First inventory what listens: use the console helper getEventListeners(node) or CDP DOMDebugger.getEventListeners on the canvas, cards and buttons, and record listener type, useCapture and the source line, which points at the handler even inside a bundle. Look for pointermove, pointerenter, pointerleave, mousemove and a requestAnimationFrame loop; a custom cursor is usually a position: fixed div with pointer-events: none, a transform driven by a lerp, and cursor: none on the body. Second, measure the motion: log the cursor element's offsetLeft or transform every frame while moving the pointer in a straight 400px line at constant speed, then solve for the damping factor in next = current + (target - current) * k and its lag time constant; for magnetic buttons sample the button transform at several pointer offsets to get attraction radius and gain falloff. Third, capture state transitions: trigger hover with Playwright and read transition-property, transition-duration and transition-timing-function plus any scale, border, background-image or text swap at 0, 50 and 100 percent of the transition. Rebuild stack: vanilla pointer events with one rAF loop writing only transform and opacity, or GSAP quickTo for the same damping, and the trail hidden for coarse pointers via matchMedia('(pointer: fine)'). Acceptance: sampled lag matches within 10 percent, hover transitions match duration and curve, interaction holds a 16.7ms frame on a throttled mobile CPU, and touch devices fall back to plain tap feedback.
