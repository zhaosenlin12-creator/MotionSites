Goal: prove which faces actually painted instead of assuming a webfont arrived. Open the reference at 1440x900 in a disposable profile and throttle to 1.6 Mbps so the swap window stays visible, then read document.fonts as a FontFaceSet: log every FontFace's family, style, weight, unicodeRange, display, status and loaded count, plus document.fonts.check() for the split headline, body and caption runs. Record the timeline: navigation start, the first frame with no text, the first frame with fallback glyphs, the first frame with the intended face, and document.fonts.ready. Compare screenshots taken 0 and 1500 ms after DOMContentLoaded: identical boxes with different glyph shapes is a FOUT signature, an empty box that fills is FOIT. For each family capture the @font-face src list, the preload or preconnect that fetched it, transfer bytes, and whether a size-adjust or metric-override declaration sits on the fallback. Derive the layout delta by measuring a 60-character line in both states to the pixel, plus the line-height difference; that number is the reservation the rebuild needs. Never substitute a licensed face you did not clear; record provenance per file. Rebuild stack: local woff2 with font-display: swap, one size-adjust value solved from the measured delta, and a preload for the single face used above the fold. Acceptance: every painted face has an observed status line, the FOUT or FOIT verdict names the two screenshots it rests on, the fallback delta is within 2 px after size-adjust, and each face without a licence is marked unknown rather than shipped.
